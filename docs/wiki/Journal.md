# Journal

The **journal** lists the brain's **episodes**: what it has been told, **exactly as it was told and in order of arrival**. Each approved proposal, note or message is an episode. From each episode the **facts** are then extracted (the [[Facts]] tab), which are the already-distilled knowledge; the episode keeps the original text in case you need to go back to it.

## What you see
Episodes are grouped by day (most recent on top). Each card shows:
- the **title** and the time;
- the episode **type** (text, message, JSON…) and how many **facts** were extracted from it;
- an excerpt of the content.

Click a card to open the **side panel** with the date, the **full text** and the **facts extracted** from that episode (invalidated ones are struck through).

## Filters
Above the list is the filter bar:
- **Search** by text or title.
- **Type** of episode (with a counter).
- **From / To** (dates).
- **Order**: most recent or oldest.
"Clear" removes all the filters. The list is paginated (25–200 per page).

## What it is for
- Checking that what you sent arrived and what facts it produced.
- Auditing where a piece of data came from.
- Spotting episodes with **0 facts**: either they had no concrete information, or extraction failed (see [[Jobs]]).

> Episodes are **immutable**: they are not edited. If something was wrong, correct it by invalidating or editing the *fact* ([[Facts]]), or add a new episode that contradicts it.
